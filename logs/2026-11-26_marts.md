## Learned
- Reminder that SELECT DISTINCT applies to all the fields after it (so whole row has to be identical to be dropped)
- CREATE OR REPLACE TABLE xxx AS
- FROM UNNEST(GENERATE_DATE_ARRAY(DATE '2015-01-01', DATE '2025-12-31', INTERVAL 1 DAY)) AS date
    - GENERATE_DATE_ARRAY(start_date, end_date, interval) generates the calendar
    - UNNEST makes it into rows
- EXTRACT(xxx FROM date) where xxx can be DAY/MONTH/YEAR/DAYOFWEEK etc
    - BQ DAYOFWEEK gives 1 for Sunday (7 for sat)
- FORMAT_DATE('%a', date) for day name. %u for ISO dayofweek, %b for month name, %Y-%m for year-month (as with strftime in SQLite)
